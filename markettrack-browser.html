Foods

Four aisles, one index — H-E-B · Walmart · Sam's Club · Joe V's

Three ways to add a food: search above, look up a package barcode, or photograph a nutrition facts label.

The most-ordered items at 22 chains around Katy — 256 items, with calories and macros from each chain's own published nutrition (researched Nov 2026). They log to a meal exactly like foods, using the “Log to meal” choice above; where a serving weight is published you can also log by grams. Pizza is per slice of a 14″ large unless the slice says otherwise.

Good to know before you log
  • Whataburger figures come from Whataburger's official nutrition PDF dated March 2021 — the most recent complete official document available. Whataburger may have tweaked some items since; its current app/website values are the final authority.
  • Wendy's small chili is 280 kcal in the current US menu panels used here, but some older pages list 170 kcal — worth a double-check in the Wendy's app.
  • Dairy Queen burgers and chicken come from DQ's official US food table dated Aug 26, 2024, read through a secondary nutrition calculator (DQ's food page itself couldn't be opened directly) — they are not freshly verified. The Blizzards come from DQ's current treat table (Sep 2026).
  • Where a chain doesn't publish a number, it's left blank — never filled in as 0. Burger King and KFC don't publish fiber at all; Wendy's, Chipotle, Torchy's and Panda Express don't publish it per item, and a few items (like Panera's Fuji Apple Chicken Salad) publish calories and protein only.
  • Chipotle bowls and burritos are the standard builds, added up from Chipotle's official per-ingredient values. Little Caesars slices are the official whole-pizza totals divided by 8, and KFC's 3 tenders are 3× the published single-tender values.

Build a shopping list from any store's foods — tap 🧺 Basket on a food card in the index. The basket totals up the calories and macros, and for each item it checks the other stores for a lower-calorie version of the same product. It stays saved in this browser until you clear it.

Every barcode you look up is saved on this device — look the same one up again later and it shows from your cache, no internet needed.

Paste a recipe link, or paste the recipe text itself. MarketTrack reads the title, servings and ingredients, turns each ingredient into a component (matched to your index when possible), and builds a food you can save and log — one serving at a time.

Two different removes: to take a food off today, go to Today and step its servings down to 0. To remove a food from your Foods index for good, tap Remove on its card in the index above — past days you've already logged stay as they are.

Your profile & target calculator

Daily targets

🥗 Diet-mode presets

One tap sets protein / carbs / fat for your current calorie target above. Calories, fiber and water stay as they are. Tap Save targets first if you just changed calories, or change calories in the preset preview — presets always use the calorie number in the box above.

🍽️ Per-meal targets

Give each meal its own calorie and macro target, on top of the daily target above. Each meal block on Today then shows its own progress (for example “Lunch 320 / 600 kcal”). Use a split button to make the four meals add up to your daily target exactly, or type your own numbers and tap Save.

“Auto” splits your daily target 25% breakfast · 30% lunch · 35% dinner · 10% snack and updates itself whenever the daily target changes. Splits put any rounding remainder on dinner, so the four meals always add up to the daily target exactly.

☁️ WebDAV sync

Sync your MarketTrack data to your own WebDAV server (for example a Nextcloud or a home server). “Sync now” uploads all of the app's data to a file called markettrack-data.json on that server; “Restore from server” downloads it and replaces what's in this browser. Your server address and login are saved only in this browser and sent only to that server. If the server doesn't allow web pages to connect (CORS), the sync can't reach it — the message below will say so plainly.

Set up free sync — Koofr

No server of your own? Koofr is free forever — 10 GB, no credit card required, and WebDAV is included on the free plan. It takes about two minutes to set up:

  1. Create a free account at koofr.eu.
  2. In Koofr, go to your account settings and generate an app password for WebDAV — use that app password here, not your main Koofr password.
  3. Enter the server URL below, your Koofr account email as the username, and the app password, then tap Sync now.

The sync file is markettrack-data.json, stored in the root of your Koofr account. Your credentials are stored only in this browser and sent only to the Koofr server.

Restore replaces everything in this browser with the server's copy — export a backup first if you're unsure. It asks you to confirm before anything is replaced.
